Security

We are committed to keeping your information secure. To prevent unauthorized access or disclosure, we maintain appropriate physical, administrative, and technological safeguards to protect the data we collect online.

Third-Party Sharing

Creative Hub Publishing values your privacy and ensures that your personal information remains protected at all times.

  • We do not sell, rent, or disclose client content or contact details to outside parties for their independent marketing or commercial use.
  • Limited technical or transaction data may be processed by necessary service providers, such as payment, security, analytics, and customer-support tools, solely to operate the website or deliver the requested service.

Use of Cookies

To improve your browsing experience and website performance, Creative Hub Publishing uses cookies and related technologies.

These small alphanumeric identifiers help our system recognize your browser and keep items saved in your shopping cart during visits. Most web browsers include a Help section that guides you on managing cookies, whether to block, disable, or receive alerts when a new one is placed.

Allowing cookies ensures full access to our website's main features and a smoother user experience. Based on our observations, most visitors prefer to keep cookies enabled.

In addition to cookies, IP addresses are logged to help diagnose server issues, manage site performance, and collect general demographic details such as location and Internet service provider. Aggregate data may also be analyzed to understand visitor behavior, search patterns, and overall site usage, none of which is linked to personally identifiable information (PII).

When combined, limited data such as browser type, access times, and referring URLs may be shared with third parties, though no personal details are ever included.

Customer Data Protection

At Creative Hub Publishing, we take your privacy seriously and follow strict data protection practices to keep your information safe.

  • Confidentiality: Your name and contact information are never shared with or disclosed to any third party. All details you provide remain confidential.
  • Purpose of Use: Information is used only for the purposes described in this policy, including responding to requests, delivering and improving services, requesting feedback, and tailoring your website experience.
  • Compliance: We fully comply with PCI and consumer data protection standards to maintain the highest level of data security.
  • Sensitive Information: Our team members are not authorized to request or store sensitive details such as credit or debit card numbers. Clients are strongly advised not to share personal or financial information directly with staff.
  • In-House Production Facilities: To maintain consistent quality and timely delivery, your project may be handled by Creative Hub Publishing team members working across our in-house production facilities. Every facility follows the same confidentiality, access-control, and data-protection requirements.
